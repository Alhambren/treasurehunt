# Landing Page Prompt for Gemini

## The Task

Create a landing page for **Treasure Hunt** — an autonomous on-chain economic game built on Base. The visual style should evoke **Monkey Island** (the classic LucasArts adventure game series) — pixel-art-inspired but modern, warm and inviting, playful but not childish.

---

## The Scene: Pirates Landing on a Beach

The hero section should depict **pirates arriving at a mysterious island shore**:

- A wooden longboat pulled onto sand
- 2-3 pirates stepping onto the beach, silhouetted or stylized
- A treasure map held by one of them, catching the light
- In the distance: a jungle interior, hints of mountains, an X somewhere suggesting buried treasure
- Warm sunset/golden hour lighting — not dark or ominous
- Caribbean palette: turquoise water, golden sand, deep green palms, warm sky

**The mood:** Discovery. Arrival. The adventure is just beginning.

---

## Visual Style Reference

**Monkey Island aesthetic:**
- Hand-painted backgrounds with visible brushwork
- Strong silhouettes and readable shapes
- Warm, saturated colors
- Slight pixel-art influence but not rigid
- Whimsical but grounded — pirates are real, but there's magic in the air
- No cartoonish goofiness — confident, knowing humor

**UI Materials (from the app):**
- Parchment textures (cream to tan, subtle noise)
- Brass accents (buttons, dividers)
- Ink typography (deep brown, weathered)
- Weathered wood frames

---

## Page Structure

### Hero Section
- Scene: Pirates landing on beach
- Headline: **"The Sea Decides. The Map Remembers."** (or similar evocative line)
- Subhead: Brief one-liner about what Treasure Hunt is
- Primary CTA: "Begin Exploration" or "Enter the Hunt"
- Secondary CTA: "Read the Map" (links to docs/how it works)

### What Is This? Section
- 3 simple cards or icons explaining:
  1. **Explore** — Contribute to the expedition. The Tides of Fortune determine your fate.
  2. **Discover** — When the chest fills, someone finds the treasure. Explorers share the spoils.
  3. **Earn** — HUNT tokens flow to contributors. Stake to join the crew's share.

### The Map Section
- Visual of the MAP token bonding curve concept
- "A long-term position. Patience rewards the cartographer."
- Simple explanation of how MAP works

### The Crew Section
- Explanation of staking HUNT
- "Stow HUNT below deck. Share in every discovery."
- 7-day cooldown visualized somehow

### Footer
- Links: Docs, Contract, Twitter/X, Discord
- Built on Base logo
- "Fair winds." as sign-off

## Tone of Voice

- **Warm, not cold** — This isn't a trading terminal
- **Playful, not silly** — Monkey Island wit, confident humor
- **Mythic, not corporate** — "The sea decides" not "Randomized outcome distribution"
- **Patient, not urgent** — No "LIMITED TIME" energy

Example copy:
> "Every expedition needs a crew. Every crew needs a ship. Every ship needs a map. Treasure Hunt is all three — an autonomous voyage where the bold explore, the patient hold, and the sea decides who finds the chest."

## Technical Notes

- Should be a single-page static site
- Responsive (mobile-first is fine)
- Smooth scroll between sections
- Subtle parallax on hero scene would be nice
- No heavy JS frameworks needed — vanilla or light framework
- Host-ready (Vercel, Netlify, etc.)

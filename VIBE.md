# Treasure Hunt — The Vibe

## The Feeling

**Monkey Island meets DeFi.** A pirate adventure game that happens to be an autonomous economic protocol. The interface should feel like you've discovered an ancient captain's desk — weathered maps, brass instruments, ink-stained ledgers, and the quiet confidence of someone who's sailed these waters before.

Not a casino. Not a trading terminal. A *voyage*.

---

## Visual DNA

### Materials
- **Parchment**: Cream to tan gradients, subtle noise texture, age stains
- **Ink**: Deep brown (#3d3210), faded where old, rich where fresh
- **Brass**: Warm gold highlights, tarnished edges, functional not decorative
- **Weathered Wood**: Dark oak frames, salt-worn edges

### Typography
- **Pirata One**: Headlines, section titles, important values — the captain's hand
- **IM Fell English**: Body text, descriptions, logs — the scholar's record

## Interaction Philosophy

### The Game vs. The Asset

**Exploration** (the game) is dynamic:
- Spinning fate wheel
- Celebration particles
- Discovery overlays
- Dramatic log entries
- "The sea decides" energy

**MAP buying** (the asset) is calm:
- Single action, no drama
- Subtle success states
- Deterministic feedback
- "A long-term position" energy
- Like buying ETH on a DEX, not playing a slot

### Cartographer's Notes
Small ink dots that reveal weathered paper scraps on hover. They explain, they don't interrupt. The cartographer annotated this map long ago — you're just discovering their notes.

- Appear below the anchor point
- Flip up if near bottom edge
- Close immediately when pointer leaves the dot
- No persistence, no click-to-pin
- Purely ephemeral

### Captain's Log
An open book artifact. Left page and right page. Entries appear as if hand-written — slight position variation, occasional marginalia (⚓, ☠). Two types of entries:

1. **Flavor line**: Monkey Island tone — *"The sea takes its share. The map darkens with ink."*
2. **Outcome line**: Plain facts — *"Result: 0× — Contributed 1.00 USDC to the expedition"*

## Language Bible (excerpts)

**Never say:**
- Bet, betting, gamble, gambling
- Jackpot, slot, spin
- Win/lose (in gambling sense)
- Odds, probability (to users)

**Always say:**
- Exploration, expedition, voyage
- Contribution (not bet)
- Discovery (not jackpot)
- The Tides of Fortune (not odds wheel)
- The sea claims / fortune smiles

**Token names:**
- HUNT: The token earned through exploration
- MAP: The cartographer's map (bonding curve token)
- Doubloons: USDC

## Key Sections

### The Treasure Chest
Progress bar showing J / M. When full, discovery triggers. Gold glow when close. The chest "rattles as it fills."

### The Cartographer's Map
Treasure map background with illustrated coastlines, islands, compass rose, X marks the spot. Pinned parchment cards show stats. The Map Table sits below — a calm place to acquire MAP.

### The Tides of Fortune
Ship-on-sea illustration. Seven possible outcomes from "The Sea Claims Its Due" (0×) to "Legendary Fortune" (10×). Not a wheel — a set of fates the sea chooses from.

### Crew Quarters
HUNT staking. "Stow HUNT Below Deck" to join the crew. 7-day cooldown to leave ("Prepare to Disembark"). Stakers share in discoveries if they've explored this expedition.

## Discovery Celebration

When treasure is found:
- Full-screen golden overlay
- Centered card with bounce
- Spinning conic gradient behind
- Particle burst (coins, sparkles, stars, gems)
- *"TREASURE DISCOVERED!"*

If you missed it (arrived after discovery):
- Smaller, no bounce
- Fewer particles
- *"TREASURE WAS FOUND! While ye were away..."*

---

## The Tone

**Warm, not cold.** This isn't a Bloomberg terminal.

**Playful, not silly.** Monkey Island wit, not cartoon chaos.

**Confident, not aggressive.** The interface knows what it is.

**Patient, not urgent.** "The map reveals itself slowly."

---

## Summary

Treasure Hunt should feel like opening a chest you found in your grandfather's attic — maps, compasses, a captain's log, brass instruments. There's a game here, yes, but also something older. Something that rewards patience and punishes nothing but impatience.

The sea decides. The map remembers. The crew shares.

*Fair winds.*
